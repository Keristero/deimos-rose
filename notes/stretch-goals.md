# More than 2 clients (high effort)
- Support more than 2 players online, just allow new players to join constantly and start once they are all ready
- the in game HUD only has room for 2 players, we would change how it works with more than 2 players.
    - the top half of the HUD would show the local player's statistics
    - the bottom half of the HUD would show another player, but two clickable arrows would be shown on the HUD allowing the player to switch which other player's stats they are looking at (if they want)
    - clients would be prompted to provide a name (compatibile with the high score table) before they initiate a connection to join a lobby, the name would be displayed beside the player in the lobby, automatically entered on the high score screen, and always displayed under the player's ship in game (while they are alive)
- The health of enemies would have to scale with the number of connected players.
- All netcode functions would need to be reevaluated with consideration for more players.
